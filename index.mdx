## 

The GB flexibility market is structurally complex. The same flexible asset — a battery, an EV charger, a building's heating system — can in principle deliver value to multiple parties through multiple mechanisms. Realising that potential requires people across the industry to share a working understanding of how the market is put together.

Elexon needs a shared reference model to have better conversations about flexibility.

This documentation is that shared reference. It is deliberately **descriptive rather than prescriptive**: it describes the market as it is, not as any one party would wish it to be.

This is based on the framing of the rule on the End-to-End process

## How to read it

Each section opens with an overview page that gives the big picture and a single diagram. Subsequent pages drill into specific aspects. Most pages take three to five minutes to read. Where a page references a concept defined elsewhere, it links to it.

If you're new to the GB flex market, read the overview pages of all four sections first. If you're looking up something specific, the [Glossary](/reference/glossary) is the fastest entry point.
