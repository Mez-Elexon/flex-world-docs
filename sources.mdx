This site is a synthesis. The authoritative sources for what's described here are listed below, grouped by category.

## Flexibility Market Rules

The Flexibility Market Rules are published at the Elexon BSC documents portal and are legally binding via licence conditions for System Operators.

- **FMR-GLO Market Facilitator Glossary** — [bscdocs.elexon.co.uk/flex-market/flexibility-market-rules/fmr-glo-market-facilitator-glossary](https://bscdocs.elexon.co.uk/flex-market/flexibility-market-rules/fmr-glo-market-facilitator-glossary). The defined terms used across all FMRs and Governance Documents. Sections 1, 2, and 3 are legally binding.
- **End-to-End Process FMR** — defines Market Phases, Market Activities, and the local definitions of Asset, Resource, Unit, Pre-Qualify, Qualification, etc.
- **Pre-Qualification Criteria FMR (FMR-PQC)** — referenced in Q.1 (commercial qualification) and Q.5/Q.6 (technical Asset qualification).
- **Product Definitions FMR (FMR-PR)** — defines the named Products: OU, PR, SAOU, SU, VAOU.

## Market Facilitator Governance Documents

- **Change Management Procedure (FGD-CMP)** — [bscdocs.elexon.co.uk/flex-market/flexibility-market-governance/fgd-cmp-change-management-procedure](https://bscdocs.elexon.co.uk/flex-market/flexibility-market-governance/fgd-cmp-change-management-procedure). The procedure governing changes to FMRs.
- **Implementation Monitoring Procedure (FGD-IMP)** — governs Implementation Monitoring and the maintenance of the Flexibility Market Catalogue.
- **Flexibility Market Rules Framework (FGD-FMF)** — the overarching structured approach for FMR definition, documentation, publication, and coordination.
- **Governance Framework Document** — issued by the Authority (Ofgem) establishing the Market Facilitator Arrangements.

## Working artefacts

- **Flexibility Market Catalogue** — the catalogue of parameters submitted by System Operators and maintained by the Market Facilitator. Submissions cover Sub-Market parameters, activity selections, dependencies, time constraints.
- **End-to-End Process FMR Reference (definitions and Market Activity descriptions)** — provides full definitions of each of the 29 Market Activities, with notes on how each has evolved.

## Regulator publications

- **Ofgem decision letter, 29 July 2024** — designating Elexon as Market Facilitator.
- **Ofgem FMAR decision document** — establishing the requirement for common asset registration.
- **Ofgem RIIO-ED2 final determinations** — DNO price control framework relevant to flexibility procurement.
- **Ofgem decisions on DSO governance and TSO–DSO coordination.**

## Government policy

- **DESNZ / Ofgem Smart Systems and Flexibility Plan.**
- **Energy Act 2023.**
- **DESNZ publications on net zero and flexibility-relevant policy.**

## Industry codes

- **Balancing and Settlement Code (BSC)** — governs the wholesale balancing and settlement framework. Operated by Elexon.
- **Distribution Connection and Use of System Agreement (DCUSA).**
- **Connection and Use of System Code (CUSC).**
- **Grid Code, Distribution Code.**
- **Smart Energy Code (SEC) / Smart Secure Energy System (SSES) framework** — relevant for the SSES-style Resource definition (Energy Smart Appliances, Customer Energy Managers).

## NESO publications

- **NESO Service Terms** — the service-specific terms covering Eligible Asset requirements, Service Parameters, and dispatch/verification arrangements for ancillary services.
- **NESO Product Catalogue** — current ancillary service products and Balancing Mechanism arrangements.

## DNO publications

Each DNO publishes its own:

- Flexibility tender notices and Product specifications.
- Constraint Managed Zone publications.
- Service Terms or operational rules layered on top of the FMRs.

## International reference models

- **Norwegian Flexibility Information System (FIS / EuroFlex)** — the international reference defining the SPG → AP → CU → TR hierarchy. See [Reference Models](/reference/reference-models) for the cross-walk to GB FMR vocabulary.

## Standards bodies

- **IEC standards** — 61970 (CIM transmission), 61968 (CIM distribution), 61850 (substation automation), 62325 (market communications).
- **OpenADR Alliance** — automated demand response specifications.

## A note on currency

The FMR-GLO is at version 1.0, published 12 December 2025. The FMR landscape is actively evolving. Where this site references specific definitions, it follows FMR-GLO v1.0; where this site discusses operational practice (Sub-Markets in scope, systems landscape), it reflects the Market Facilitator's working view as captured in working artefacts shared with the documentation maintainer.

This site will be updated as published sources change. Where the published FMRs disagree with this site, the published FMRs win.
