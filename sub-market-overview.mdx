## Observations on the catalogue

A few patterns are worth flagging:

**The same Product appears across many Sub-Markets.** Scheduled Utilisation alone has eight Sub-Markets across six DNOs. This is the current 1:1 product-to-Sub-Market shape where each operator's procurement of the same Product is treated as a separate Sub-Market.

**Sub-Product handling is consistent for symmetric Products.** Where directional symmetry exists (Dynamic Containment High/Low, Quick Reserve Positive/Negative), the convention is one Sub-Market with two Sub-Products, not two separate Sub-Markets.

**Naming is inconsistent.** "Scheduled Utilisation", "Scheduled Utilisation (SU)", "Long Term Scheduled Utilisation", "Scheduled Utilisation — Long Term" all appear in the catalogue. Rationalising naming is part of the Market Facilitator's coordination scope.

**Some markets are flagged out of scope.** Reactive, Stability, and ANM are listed but not currently in MF scope. Capacity Market and Wholesale Market are listed for completeness but governed by separate frameworks (DESNZ, BSC parties); the Wholesale Market is flagged as in scope from 2028 in the second delivery plan.
