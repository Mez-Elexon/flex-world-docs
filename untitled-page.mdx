## Domain entities

**Service Point Group (SPG).** A grouping of Accounting Points used for portfolio-level participation and reporting. See [Asset Hierarchy](/assets/hierarchy).

**Accounting Point (AP).** A defined point in the network at which energy is metered for settlement. The settlement boundary. Identified in GB by MPAN (or MSID in some contexts). See [Accounting Points](/assets/accounting-points).

**Asset.** A physical or aggregated entity registered by an FSP for participation in flexibility markets. Sits behind one or more APs and contains one or more Controllable Units.

**Controllable Unit (CU).** The dispatch-addressable level of the asset hierarchy — what receives instructions and reports performance. _Alias: Meterable Unit (used in some Elexon documentation)._ See [Controllable Units](/assets/controllable-units).

**Technical Resource (TR).** The lowest-level technical component within a CU.

**Site.** A physical location with one or more APs and Assets.

## Actors

**Flexibility Service Provider (FSP).** The party contracting with a system operator to deliver flexibility. Owns the relationship with one or more Asset Owners. See [Service Providers](/actors/service-providers).

**Distribution Network Operator (DNO).** The licensed operator of a regional distribution network. See [System Operators](/actors/system-operators).

**Distribution System Operator (DSO).** The active-network role increasingly performed by DNOs, including local flexibility procurement.

**National Energy System Operator (NESO).** The operator of the GB transmission system. See [System Operators](/actors/system-operators).

**Asset Owner.** The legal owner of a flexible asset.

**Agent / Installer.** A third party empowered by an Asset Owner to register, update, or operate the asset on the Owner's behalf.

**Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM).** Manufacturers of the physical equipment that delivers flexibility (battery makers, EV charger vendors, heat pump producers).

**Market Platform.** A digital platform on which flexibility products are bought and sold. Examples include Piclo, NodeES, and Flexible Power.

**Market Facilitator (MF).** Elexon, designated by Ofgem to provide neutral coordinating infrastructure. See [Platforms and Facilitator](/actors/platforms-and-facilitator).

**Ofgem.** The Office of Gas and Electricity Markets — GB's energy regulator. See [Regulator](/actors/regulator).

**DESNZ.** Department for Energy Security and Net Zero — UK government department setting energy policy.

## Market mechanics

**Sub-Market.** A specific procurement vehicle parameterised by product, contract duration, geography, and other dimensions. See [Sub-Markets](/mechanics/sub-markets).

**Product.** A defined flexibility service that a system operator procures. Examples: Scheduled Utilisation, Dynamic Containment.

**System Need.** A network or system requirement that a flexibility product addresses.

**Constraint Management Zone (CMZ).** A geographic area within which flexibility is required to manage a network constraint.

**Bid.** An offer submitted by an FSP in response to a Buy Requirement.

**Trade.** A matched buy and sell — the outcome of market clearing.

**Baseline Method.** The agreed method for calculating counterfactual consumption against which delivered flexibility is measured.

**Dispatch Signal / Utilisation Signal.** The instruction to a CU to deliver flexibility.

**Cease Signal.** The instruction to a CU to return to baseline.

## Lifecycle phases

**Exploration.** Pre-market activity to identify needs and define products. See [Phases](/lifecycle/phases).

**Qualification.** Registration and pre-qualification of FSPs, Assets, and CUs.

**Competition.** Buy/sell signalling and market clearing.

**Scheduling and Dispatch.** Operational delivery.

**Verification.** Performance assessment.

**Settlement.** Invoicing and payment.

## Standards and infrastructure

**FMAR (Flexibility Market Asset Registration).** The programme establishing common asset registration infrastructure across GB.

**Flexibility Market Catalogue (FMC).** The submission template through which system operators register their sub-markets with the Market Facilitator. See [Standards](/reference/standards).

**Data Sharing Infrastructure (DSI).** National data exchange infrastructure that flexibility market APIs increasingly align to.

**IES5.** Information Exchange Standard, version 5.

**CIM (Common Information Model).** International standard for power system information exchange (IEC 61970, IEC 61968).

**OpenADR.** Open Automated Demand Response standard.

**FIS / EuroFlex.** Norwegian Flexibility Information System — international reference model defining the four-level asset hierarchy (SPG → AP → CU → TR).

## Identifiers

**MPAN (Meter Point Administration Number).** The standard identifier for an electricity supply point in GB.

**MSID (Metering System Identifier).** Identifier used in the Balancing and Settlement Code context.

**EAN.** European Article Number — used in some EU flexibility contexts; not the primary GB identifier.
