This glossary brings together two layers of definition:

- **Global definitions** — terms defined in the [Market Facilitator Glossary (FMR-GLO)](https://bscdocs.elexon.co.uk/flex-market/flexibility-market-rules/fmr-glo-market-facilitator-glossary), the Flexibility Market Rule that establishes common terminology across all FMRs and Governance Documents. Sections 1, 2 and 3 of FMR-GLO are **legally binding** on System Operators via licence conditions.
- **Local definitions** — terms defined in specific FMRs (most relevant here: the End-to-End Process FMR) for use in their own context.

Where a term carries a legal definition, this site uses it verbatim. Where a term is operationally common but not formally defined, that's noted.

## A — F

**Asset** _(Local definition — End-to-End Process FMR)._ A generalisation of DER (in FMR-PQC) and Eligible Asset (in NESO Service Terms) — relates to a logical concept to deliver the overall usable flexibility, involving Resources together with command and control, telecommunications, and local and/or remote energy management system. **An Asset represents one or more Resources that are controlled together at a single network connection point.** See [Assets: Overview](/assets/overview).

**Authority** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The Gas and Electricity Markets Authority established by section 1(1) of the Utilities Act 2000. In practice, this is **Ofgem**.

**Auction Contract Length.** The duration of a contract awarded through a specific competitive process. Operationally common; not separately defined in FMR-GLO.

**Bid.** An offer submitted by a Service Provider in response to Buy Requirements. Operationally common; specific definitions vary by Sub-Market.

**Buy Requirements.** The system operator's published need within a competitive process — what they want to procure, when, and where.

**Constraint Managed Zone (CMZ).** A geographic area within which flexibility is required to manage a network constraint. Operationally common in DNO Sub-Markets.

**Contract Award** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The execution and award by the Company of a contract for the provision of Flexibility Services by the Provider.

**Distribution Licence** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A licence issued under section 6(1)(c) of the Electricity Act 1989, with the exception of licences issued to Independent Distribution Network Operators.

**Distribution Network Operator (DNO)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A party which holds a Distribution Licence in respect of distribution activities in Great Britain, acting in that capacity. See [System Operators](/actors/system-operators).

**Flexibility** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ Modifying generation and/or consumption patterns in reaction to an external signal (such as a change in price) to provide a service within the energy system.

**Flexibility Market** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ Markets for services that provide Flexibility to the electricity system.

**Flexibility Market Asset Registration (FMAR)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ Common digital infrastructure enabling the collection, storage and access of asset registration data for local and national flexibility markets, covering technical asset data and flexibility service data.

**Flexibility Market Catalogue (FMC)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The catalogue of relevant parameters submitted by System Operators in accordance with the Flexibility Market Rules and maintained by the Market Facilitator under FGD-IMP Implementation Monitoring Procedure. See [Sub-Market Catalogue](/reference/sub-market-catalogue).

**Flexibility Market Rule (FMR)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The set of rules of that name (inclusive of rules in respect of Flexibility Market Asset Registration) owned and amended by the Market Facilitator from time to time and developed in collaboration with the other licensees, through the FGD-CPC Change Management Procedure.

**Flexibility Service** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ Services provided by market participants that can adjust electricity consumption or generation in response to signals.

**Flexibility Service Provider (FSP)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ Any person providing Flexibility Services. The Local definition (End-to-End Process FMR) clarifies that FSP is "an umbrella term for the party who takes delivery and other contractual risks when providing Flexibility Services." See [Service Providers](/actors/service-providers).

## G — N

**Global Definition** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A definition set out in the Market Facilitator Glossary.

**Governance Documents** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The suite of governance documents established and maintained by the Market Facilitator which set out the processes and procedures it shall follow in discharging its responsibilities under the Governance Framework Document.

**Implementation Date** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The date on which an Approved Solution shall take operational effect, by which any affected System Operators shall have implemented any necessary system or process changes to comply.

**Implementation Monitoring** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The structured processes undertaken by the Market Facilitator to verify whether System Operators have implemented and are complying with the Flexibility Market Rules; and to populate the Flexibility Market Catalogue.

**Independent Distribution Network Operator (IDNO)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A company licensed to maintain and manage electricity distribution networks in Great Britain. **Note:** IDNOs are explicitly excluded from the FMR-GLO definition of DNO.

**Industry Code** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The formalised rules approved by the Authority that support the electricity industry to supply energy to homes and businesses.

**Innovation Trial** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A time-limited and scope-limited trial of a Sub-Market established for the purpose of research, development, or testing.

**Local Definition** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A definition set out in a Flexibility Market Rule or Market Facilitator Governance Document.

**Market Activity** _(Local — End-to-End Process FMR)._ A discrete, operational step carried out by a participant or operator that contributes to a broader market process. **Market Activity can occur in parallel or sequentially to one another.** See [Activity Dependencies](/lifecycle/activity-dependencies).

**Market Facilitator** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The Market Facilitator role performed in accordance with the Authority's decision letter dated 29 July 2024 and pursuant to the Governance Framework Document. The role is performed by **Elexon**.

**Market Facilitator Arrangements** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The mechanisms, roles, rules and responsibilities set out in the Governance Framework Document.

**Market Facilitator Glossary** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The Flexibility Market Rule with the document identifier FMR-GLO, which sets out the defined terms across the Flexibility Market Rules and Market Facilitator Governance Documents.

**Market Facilitator Rules** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The Market Facilitator Governance Documents and the Flexibility Market Rules.

**Market Information** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The quantitative and qualitative data, feedback, and insights collected by the Market Facilitator from System Operators and other stakeholders to support its functions.

**Market Phase** _(Local — End-to-End Process FMR)._ A logical grouping of Market Activities that share a common objective and temporal alignment in the end-to-end process. See [Lifecycle Phases](/lifecycle/phases).

**National Energy System Operator (NESO)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ National Energy System Operator Limited (company number: 11014226) (and any successor to its role) established under the Energy Act 2023. See [System Operators](/actors/system-operators).

## O — R

**Operational Utilisation (OU)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The product of that name as defined in the Product Definitions Flexibility Market Rule.

**Peak Reduction (PR)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The product of that name as defined in the Product Definitions Flexibility Market Rule.

**Pre-Qualify** _(Local — End-to-End Process FMR)._ A process conducted ex-ante to determine whether a participant, asset, or unit meets the minimum technical, operational, commercial, and data-exchange requirements necessary to participate in a flexibility Sub-Market.

**Product.** A defined flexibility service that a System Operator procures. The five products currently named in FMR-GLO are: Operational Utilisation, Peak Reduction, Scheduled Availability \+ Operational Utilisation, Scheduled Utilisation, Variable Availability \+ Operational Utilisation. Each is defined in the Product Definitions FMR. See [Needs and Products](/mechanics/needs-and-products).

**Publish Date** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The date on which an approved Proposed Solution to the Market Facilitator Rules shall be published and take legal effect.

**Qualification** _(Local — End-to-End Process FMR)._ The state of a Service Provider, Asset, or Unit being formally recognised as eligible and compliant to participate in a Sub-Market, having satisfied all applicable pre-qualification criteria and subsequently demonstrated compliance through verified performance. _Note: Qualification is also used to describe the Market Phase inclusive of the Activities needed to achieve the state of Qualification._

**Resource** _(Local — End-to-End Process FMR)._ The physical Plant/Apparatus (BSC) or devices (e.g., Energy Smart Appliance/Customer Energy Manager (SSES)) that generate, consume or store electricity. **A Resource needs to be paired by an accountable Service Provider with an approved Metering System to become a registered Asset in a relevant Sub-Market.** See [Resources](/assets/resources).

## S — Z

**Scheduled Availability \+ Operational Utilisation (SAOU)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The product of that name as defined in the Product Definitions Flexibility Market Rule.

**Scheduled Utilisation (SU)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The product of that name as defined in the Product Definitions Flexibility Market Rule.

**Sell Requirements.** A Service Provider's submission within a competitive process — the bid/offer in response to Buy Requirements.

**Service Provider.** See **Flexibility Service Provider**.

**Strategic Leadership** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The Market Facilitator's role in providing ongoing thought leadership, strategic advice, and industry engagement to support the effective development and operation of Flexibility Markets. Includes horizon-scanning, advising the Authority and DESNZ, developing the Delivery Plan, and identifying barriers to flexibility.

**Sub-Market** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A specific segment of the overall Flexibility Market, distinguished by **a specific System Operator, procuring a specific product within a specific time period.** A Sub-market may be built of multiple distinct geographic zones, operating in different directions, clearing different volumes at different prices. See [Sub-Markets](/mechanics/sub-markets).

**Sub-Product.** A directional or symmetric variant of a Product within the same Sub-Market. Examples include Dynamic Containment High and Low, or Quick Reserve Positive and Negative. Operationally common; not separately defined in FMR-GLO.

**System Operator** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ Entities responsible for the operation and coordination of electricity systems and markets. Specifically: **National Energy System Operator (NESO) and Distribution Network Operators (DNOs).** See [System Operators](/actors/system-operators).

**Trade.** A matched buy and sell — the contractual outcome of market clearing.

**Unit** _(Local — End-to-End Process FMR)._ A generalisation of Auction Unit (NESO) which may contain a single Asset or an Asset Group (DNO) to be used for a product procured via / delivered in a Sub-Market. See [Units](/assets/units).

**Utilisation Signal.** The instruction that initiates the delivery of utilisation by a Unit. Operationally common.

**Variable Availability \+ Operational Utilisation (VAOU)** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ The product of that name as defined in the Product Definitions Flexibility Market Rule.

**Variant.** A configurable parameter of a Product or Sub-Market (e.g. directional symmetry, specific delivery window).

**Working Day** _(Global — FMR-GLO)._ A day (other than a Saturday or a Sunday) on which banks are open in London for general interbank business in Sterling.

## A note on terminology evolution

The FMR-GLO is a living document. Version 1.0 was published 12 December 2025 and includes a relatively small set of Global definitions; the explicit intent is to grow this set over time and reduce reliance on Local definitions across individual FMRs.

Several concepts in active operational use — Sub-Product, Auction Contract Length, Bid, Buy/Sell Requirements, Trade, Utilisation Signal — are not yet formally defined. They appear here to support shared understanding, but the formal terminology landscape will continue to evolve.
